## In one paragraph

Lazurio Example Organization finds online mechanisms that demonstrably earn money, adapts them legally under its own brand, tests them small and scales only what has evidence. The brand therefore looks like a calm instrument panel: graphite surfaces that stay out of the way and one signal blue for the action that moves work forward. Nothing glows for decoration; if something stands out, it carries information. The Organization and its brand are invented: it is Lazurio's public reference Organization.

## Principles

1. **Evidence first.** Numbers, sources and dates are visible, aligned and easy to compare. A claim without evidence is shown as a hypothesis.
2. **One action per view.** Each screen or slide has at most one primary action in signal blue. Everything else is secondary.
3. **Attention is rare.** What changed since the last review is marked with the soft accent, never a whole card.
4. **Quiet by default.** Generous spacing, hairlines instead of heavy borders, no gradients and no shadows beyond the floating layer.
5. **Status is a word, not only a colour.** Every coloured status also says what it is ("Hypothesis", "Verified signal").

## Logo

The symbol is three bars and one signal dot: a shorthand for the letter E of Example and "a full stop after the matter". The name stands beside the symbol in the heading face. The symbol draws in the text colour, so it works in the light and the dark theme; the dot takes the accent.

## Imagery

Real product screens, charts and diagrams rather than stock photos. Charts use the status roles for status and the accent for the one series that matters. No stock people, no money imagery (coins, banknotes, rockets) and no "get rich" metaphors.
