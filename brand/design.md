Lazurio Example Organization is Lazurio's public reference Organization; its brand is invented. It looks like a calm instrument panel: graphite surfaces that stay out of the way, hairlines, and one signal blue for the action that moves work forward, with at most one such action per view. Nothing glows for decoration; status is always a word as well as a colour. Its logo is three bars and one signal dot beside the name in the heading face, always whole and unchanged, drawn in the text colour.
