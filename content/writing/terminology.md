# Terminology

The vocabulary of the Organization is defined in `GLOSSARY.md` in the
Organization root; this list is how it appears in interfaces and documents.
When they differ, `GLOSSARY.md` wins.

## Use

| Term | Meaning | Not |
|---|---|---|
| **Mechanism** | Something online that demonstrably earns money | Hack, trick, opportunity |
| **Adaptation** | The legal version of a mechanism under our own brand | Copy, clone |
| **Hypothesis** | A claim without evidence yet | Insight, fact |
| **Experiment** | The smallest measurable test of a hypothesis | Project, campaign |
| **Metric** | The one number that decides the experiment | KPI soup |
| **Stop condition** | The date or threshold at which the experiment ends | Deadline |
| **Verified signal** | Real evidence that meets the metric | Proven, validated, guaranteed |
| **Guardrail** | A protection against a legal, platform, money or reputation risk | Restriction |
| **Stop or scale decision** | The decision recorded when an experiment ends | Review, retro |
| **Playbook** | A verified procedure that can be repeated | Best practice |
| **Draft** | Prepared work that nobody else sees yet | Pending, WIP |
| **Publish** | The one act that makes work visible or permanent | Submit, send, go live |
| **Organization** | Lazurio Example Organization as a whole | Company workspace |

## Avoid

"Guaranteed income", "passive income", "get rich", "growth hack", "viral",
"secret", "insane results", fake urgency ("only today"), and any phrase that
sounds like another company's slogan.

## Spelling

- Lazurio Example Organization in full on first mention, then "the
  Organization"; "Example" only as the short wordmark.
- Dates: "12 Oct 2026" in text, ISO `2026-10-12` in data and file names.
- Percent with a space: "3.1 %". Currency after the amount: "[amount] EUR".
