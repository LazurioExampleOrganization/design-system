# Voice and tone

The source of truth for tone is the Organization's voice guide,
`company/agents/voice/EXAMPLE_BASELINE_VOICE.md` in the Organization root. This
page applies it to interfaces, documents and e-mails. When the two disagree,
the voice guide wins and this page is updated.

## The voice

A **calm analytical operator**: concrete, without hype and without fog. The
voice helps the reader tell apart the mechanism, the evidence, the risk and the
next test.

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Matter-of-fact | Dry or bureaucratic |
| Curious | Credulous |
| Practical | Vague |
| Careful with money, law and reputation | Fearful or evasive |

## Tone by situation

| Situation | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Neutral information | Short and plain | "12 experiments, 3 with a verified signal." |
| Success | Factual, no celebration | "Decision published. The experiment is stopped." |
| Warning | Direct, names the risk | "This budget exceeds the stop condition by 40 %." |
| Error | Calm, says what to do | "The source could not be read. Try again, or add it by hand." |
| Irreversible action | Precise about consequences | "Publishing sends the summary to 4 people. It can't be unsent." |

## Do and don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Hypothesis: buyers compare prices on mobile." | "Buyers obviously compare prices on mobile." |
| "Verified signal: 3.1 % conversion, 1,240 visits, 12 Oct." | "Great results!" |
| "Stop condition reached. Stop or scale?" | "Uh-oh, something went wrong with your experiment 😬" |
| "Save draft" / "Publish" | "Submit" / "Go!" |
| "No experiments yet. Start with a mechanism from research." | "Nothing to see here." |
| "Budget [amount] per week" | "Guaranteed income", "earn fast", fake urgency |

## Words we use and avoid

See [`../writing/terminology.md`](../writing/terminology.md) for the full
list. The four that carry the method: **hypothesis**, **verified signal**,
**guardrail** and **stop or scale decision**.
