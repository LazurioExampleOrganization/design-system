## When

Illustrations belong where people see something for the first time or where it is done: an empty list at the start, nothing found, all done. In everyday work an illustration is too much; an empty filter result is marked with an icon.

## How they look

A simple line of width 2, round caps, areas in `surface` and one detail in the accent. Illustrations draw in `currentColor` and read their colours from the tokens, so they work in the light and the dark theme.
