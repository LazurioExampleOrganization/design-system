## What it is for

The design system holds together everything people see of Example: colour, type, space, motion, icons and the components screens are made of. Whoever builds a website, an App or a presentation invents nothing from scratch. They take finished parts and the rules for when to use which.

Agents use it the same way. Before they design anything, they read the [principles](principles/), [voice and writing](voice/) and the page of the component they need.

## How it is built

- **The brand** is in one place: name, palette, fonts, logo and illustrations. An Organization replaces it there and everything else follows.
- **Tokens** name values by purpose (`ink`, `surface`, `space-16`). One source gives the variables for CSS and TypeScript, for the light and the dark theme.
- **Components** exist for Astro and React with the same properties and the same HTML. Websites and knowledge bases take them in Astro, Apps in React.

Example is an invented company. Every name, figure and customer on these pages is fictional.
