dont:
  - "Do not use capitals or small labels above a heading."

## Calm is the brand

White and paper surfaces, hairlines and one accent. Expression appears in a few deliberate places, never as decoration. When unsure, take something away.

## One main action

Each screen has at most one primary button: black in the light theme, white in the dark. People should know the next step without thinking. Everything else is secondary (white with a line), soft (grey) or quiet (no background).

## A state is never colour alone

Success, warning, error and information have their colours, but colour alone is not enough: some people do not tell colours apart, and some pairs merge in poor light. A state therefore always carries colour, icon and word.

## Less text, more of the thing

A section stands on a piece of the product, a table or a picture; the text only names it. Headings are short, a paragraph holds one thought. Where the content speaks for itself, the heading is left out.

## Values only from tokens

Colour, type size, space, radius and the duration of motion each have a token. A value written straight into a page is a bug: it breaks the dark theme and the brand swap. When a token is missing, propose it to the design system.
