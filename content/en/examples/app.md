## What is here

Side navigation with the current page, breadcrumbs, a heading with the one primary action, summary cards, an alert with an action, tabs with a count, and a table of invoices with states. None of it has a style of its own: everything is components and tokens.

The primary action "New invoice" is the only black one on the screen. An invoice's state is a badge with an icon and a word, amounts are aligned to the right in tabular figures, and dates follow the page's language.
